Smart Home IoT Management & Energy Dashboard

A connected smart-home experience for managing rooms, devices, automation, and household energy consumption.

  • Role: UI/UX Designer
  • Project type: Internship • Team Project
  • Tools: Figma • FigJam
  • Platform: Mobile App / Smart Home Experience
Open Figma file

Overview

A centralized smart-home application where users can organize rooms, connect devices, monitor device statuses, and understand energy consumption patterns at both device and room levels.

Problem

  • Managing many smart devices across rooms can be confusing.
  • Users need clear visibility into device status and energy consumption.
  • Grouping and organizing devices is often inconsistent across platforms.

Goal

Create a room-first experience that allows users to manage connected devices while providing clear energy insights and simple device management flows.

Device Ecosystem

Home → Rooms → Devices. Supported devices include lights, cameras, fans, ACs, TVs, refrigerators and other connected devices.

User Flow

Home → Select Room → View Room → Add Device → Configure Device → Group Device → Monitor Device → Energy Insights

My Contributions

UI/UX Designer — Team Project

  • Room Overview, Create/Edit Room flows
  • Add Device and Configure Device flows
  • Device grouping and Device Consumption views
  • Energy Dashboard and Insights

Design System

Typography, cards, device icons, navigation and status indicators were designed to be clear on mobile while scaling to tablet and larger displays.

Outcome

The experience brings rooms, connected devices, and energy information into a single interface, reducing complexity while helping users understand and manage their smart-home environment.

Reflection — What I learned

Designing IoT experiences highlighted the importance of scalable information architecture, clear device states, and concise visualizations for energy data.
