B2B Influencer & Sponsorship Management Dashboard

A centralized B2B platform for managing creators, brands, sponsorships, campaigns, deliverables, invoices, analytics, and reporting.

  • Role: UI/UX Designer
  • Project type: Internship • Team Project
  • Tools: Figma • FigJam
  • Platform: Web Dashboard
Open Figma file

Overview

This product helps teams manage influencer/creator partnerships, brands, sponsorships, campaigns, deliverables, invoices, analytics and calendars from a centralized platform. Typical users include brand managers, creator managers, and campaign coordinators who need to coordinate partnerships and track outcomes without switching between many tools.

Problem

  • Managing creators across campaigns is fragmented across tools.
  • Tracking sponsorship progress and deliverables requires manual coordination.
  • Creating and tracking invoices and reports is time-consuming.

Goal

Create a centralized, scalable dashboard that helps teams manage the entire sponsorship workflow efficiently — from discovery to invoices and reporting.

Information Architecture

Dashboard
├── Analytics
├── Sponsorship CRM
├── Creators
├── Brands
├── Deliverables
├── Invoices
├── Reports
├── Calendar
├── Notifications
└── Settings

User Flow

Dashboard → Sponsorship CRM → Select Sponsorship → Brand / Creator → Deliverables → Invoice → Reports / Analytics

My Contributions

UI/UX Designer — Team Project

  • Creator List & Profile: Designed list views and profile layouts for quick discovery and relationship context.
  • Brand Directory: Organized brand metadata and quick access to partnership history.
  • Sponsorship CRM & Deliverables: Designed workflows to track deliverables, status, and approvals.
  • Create Invoice: Created clear form hierarchy and review flow for invoice creation.

Design System

Typography, colors, cards, buttons, tables and status indicators were designed to align with dashboard patterns used across the product. Components were created for reuse across analytics, lists and detail screens.

Outcome

The resulting dashboard structure provides a more organized way to manage creator relationships, brand partnerships, sponsorship workflows, deliverables, invoices, and reporting within a unified B2B experience.

Reflection — What I learned

Designing complex B2B workflows emphasized the importance of information architecture, reusable components for data-heavy interfaces, and balancing business needs with usability.
